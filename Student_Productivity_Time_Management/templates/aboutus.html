About StudyAI

An intelligent student productivity & time management platform designed to help students overcome academic overwhelm, master scheduling, and build lifelong productive study habits.

Why Student Productivity Matters

Modern students often struggle to balance coursework, complex projects, exam prep, and extracurricular commitments simultaneously. Without smart tools, deadline pressure leads to burnout, procrastination, and dropped performance.

StudyAI solves this challenge: By leveraging custom rule-based artificial intelligence, the platform automatically structures schedules, surfaces urgent priorities, and provides actionable time-management guidance to optimize student success.

  • ✓ Balances study sessions & break times
  • ✓ Tracks long-term semester goals
